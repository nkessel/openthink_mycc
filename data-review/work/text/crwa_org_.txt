A CLEAN, RESILIENT CHARLES RIVER.
CLIMATE RESILIENCE · ENVIRONMENTAL JUSTICE · NATURE-BASED SOLUTIONS
Since 1965, Charles River Watershed Association has been the voice of the river.
We work to protect, restore, and enhance the Charles River and its watershed using science, advocacy, and the law by developing science-based strategies to increase resilience, protect public health, and promote environmental equity as we face a changing climate, together.

It was the same year CRWA was founded, and The Standells released their iconic song “Dirty Water”. Now, 61 years later, Lee returns to the Charles to compete in the world’s largest three-day regatta once again. We sat down with Lee ahead of the 2026 HOCR to learn more about his lifetime of rowing.
