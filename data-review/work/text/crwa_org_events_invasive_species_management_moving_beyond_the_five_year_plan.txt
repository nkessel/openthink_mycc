Join us to discuss the future of invasive plant management in the Charles River!
Since 2022, Charles River Watershed Association (CRWA) and Massachusetts Department of Conservation and Recreation (DCR) have worked together to implement the Charles River Aquatic Vegetation Management Plan, and we’ve seen a significant reduction in invasive growth. With the 5-year plan coming to a close, join our public meeting to hear future plans and continued aquatic invasive management.
Bring your questions and invite your fellow boaters to learn how we can all help keep the current clear of invasive plants!
