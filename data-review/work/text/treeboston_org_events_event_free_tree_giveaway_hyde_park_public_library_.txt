Free Tree Giveaway - Hyde Park Public Library - Tree Boston
In partnership with Keep Hyde Park Beautiful and local artist Jane Marsching, join Tree Boston for our next FREE Tree Giveaway at the Hyde Park Public Library in Hyde Park!
We will be giving away 50 free trees to local residents.
Pre-registration is strongly recommended. Please fill out the form to register below. Trees are given out on a first-come first-serve basis starting at 10AM.
Priority is given to local/Boston residents.
The following tree species will be available:
1. Serviceberry
2. Magnolia
3. Eastern Redbud
4. Carolina Silverbell
5. Red Maple
More information about tree species selection will be available at the event.
