Join the first Metro Boston Restoration Partnership Meeting on November 16!
METROBOSTON
Nov 4
Written by
Carly Sherman
In the first year of this partnership (July 2026 - June 2027), we will focus on developing a strategic plan to organize, identify, and plan future restoration work across our shared watersheds.
We have scheduled our first virtual meeting for Monday, November 16th, from 4 - 5 PM.
Join us to hear an overview of the partnership, review project types and prioritization approaches, and participate in a discussion on next steps. Stipends are available upon request if they would allow you to participate fully. Feel free to share the invite with any interested individuals or community groups!
