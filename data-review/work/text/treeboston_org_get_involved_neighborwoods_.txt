Every spring and fall we plant trees in private yards—for free!
Add to the green space around you through our NeighborWoods program. We partner with residents in Boston’s environmental justice communities to plant shade trees at homes or businesses.
Every tree planted helps increase the tree canopy, which reduces the heat island effect, cleans the air, and brings natural beauty to the neighborhood.
Please note that we are currently focused on planting in these communities: Dorchester, Hyde Park, Roxbury, and Mattapan.
How NeighborWoods Works
It's easy to request a tree!
Submit your application
Homeowners: Submit the application below.
Renters: You will need permission from the owner
If your application is approved, we'll set up a site visit with you to:
Recommend the best tree(s) for your location.
Review how to care for your tree.
Ask you (or the homeowner) to sign a 2-year maintenance plan for your tree.
Agree on a time and date for planting.
No Application Fee
This program is intended to address inequity in the distribution of Boston's tree canopy and is entirely free.
Program Requirements
Each site is limited to four free trees, based on the size of the planting area.
We have a limited number of trees and staff, so we appreciate your patience.
All land/homeowners are required to sign a 2
-year Tree Maintenance Agreement
to care for and water the tree.
Apply Below
Please note – this program is available to Boston residents and Boston properties only, with a preference given to residents in Environmental Justice neighborhoods.
