How it works

Pay monthly & we buy Class 1 or "new" clean energy for you and thousands of others
We collectively add more renewables to the New England power grid
Learn more about Green Powered
Clean energy resources supported by Green Powered members.
How much it costs
Green Powered 100%
Who is eligible
Rhode Island Energy electric customers
Your electricity comes from:
100% wind power from New England wind turbines (RI "new")
Price per kWh you use:
Average home pays $30/month. 5 cents/kWh (in addition to your normal electricity charges)
Green Powered 25%
Who is eligible
Rhode Island Energy electric customers
Your electricity comes from:
25% mix of New England wind, solar, hydro, and anaerobic digestor gas (Class 1)
Price per kWh you use:
Average home pays $14/month. 2.2 cents/kWh (in addition to your normal electricity charges)
Green Powered Subscription
Who is eligible
Anyone, anywhere
Your electricity comes from:
A mix of New England wind, solar, hydro, and anaerobic digestor gas (Class 1)
Price per kWh you use:
You choose to match a specific amount of kilowatt hours per month, and the cost is always the same, billed to a credit card. The cost breaks down to $0.054/kWh.
Best (Match Most or All - 600 kWh): $32.40 per month
Good (Match about half - 300 kWh): $16.20 per month
Custom (choose your amount in 100 kWh blocks): $5.40 per block, per month 5.4 cents/kWh (in addition to your normal electricity charges. This includes processing fees)
Community Choice Electricity
Who is eligible
Residents of
these communities
Your electricity comes from:
Your town may offer a local program with a mix of wind, solar, hydro, and digester gas via Green Powered. Most offer 50% & 100% options.
Price per kWh you use:
If your community is on this
list
, you can find pricing information on their website (provided on the list).
Tax benefit
Although it is an additional cost on top of what your already pay for electricity, your payments for Green Powered (and Community Choice Electricity programs supplied by Green Energy Consumers Alliance) are considered a public good and are 100% federally tax-deductible.
2025 Tax Receipts
Your payments for Green Powered, above and beyond what you pay the utility for your electricity supply, are federally tax-deductible as charitable contributions.
After January 15th, you may download your receipt at
GreenEnergyConsumers.org/MyAccount
, or contact
[email]
to request it be sent within 2 business days.
If you are a resident of one of the Community Choice Electricity programs we serve, you may be eligible for an additional tax receipt. Receipts will be available for download by January 31, 2026, at
GreenEnergyConsumers.org/TaxReceipts
.
