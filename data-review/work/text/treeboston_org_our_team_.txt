Our Team - Tree Boston
Our Staff
David Meshoulam (he/him)
Co-Founder & Executive Director
David (pronounced Dah-veed) is Co-Founder of Tree Boston and currently serves as its Executive Director. Prior to founding the organization, David was a science educator at the high school and collegiate levels where he engaged students in expanding their understanding of science through the lens of politics, sociology, and history. A life-long environmentalist, his passion lies at the intersection of science, history, democracy, justice, and the environment. He holds a BA in Philosophy of Science, an MA in History of Science, and a PhD in Science Education, Curriculum and Instruction. He is a Senior Fellow at the Environmental Leadership Program, a graduate of the Green Communities Leadership Institute, and served as co-chair of the Urban Ecology Collaborative. Jerel Ferguson (he/him)
Director of Community Outreach & Engagement
Jerel has a profound love of nature, trees, and the Great Outdoors. A true tree hugger, he encourages urban youth and families to participate in and experience an active life in the outdoors by hiking, camping, and more. Jerel is an herbalist, a forager, and an established outdoor leader. He has a passion for growing and cooking food, he's also an urban farmer and gardener.
Lisa Crist (she/her)
Director of Advancement
Lisa is a seasoned communications and fundraising professional with a background in strategic marketing and nonprofit development. She has worked in both the public and private sectors, including roles at Harvard Business Publishing and the Los Angeles Times, and co-founded a consultancy focused on independent schools. Lisa holds an MBA in Marketing from Boston University and a bachelor’s degree from Earlham College. She loves the energy of a city and also enjoys hiking with her family.
Fitgee Jean-Pierre (he/him)
Director of Finance
Fitgee was born and raised in Boston, is a Boston College alumni and member of the Janey Scholars. He graduated in 2022 with degrees in both business and African Diaspora studies. He is a former member of FACES, an anti-racism group based in Boston College and was an administrator of Black at Boston College, an online forum allowing Black students to share their stories of discrimination. He was previously interned at Loomis Sayles, where he then took on a full-time position as a Portfolio Analyst
René LaPointe Jameson (she/her)
Director of Programs
René is a community organizer and environmental engineer. Passionate about health equity and environmental justice, René received her BS in Environmental Engineering from Tufts University. Today, René conducts research and manages programming centered around advocacy for and with marginalized communities disproportionately exposed to environmental hazards. This has included Indigenous peoples, other communities of color, refugees, people currently or formerly incarcerated, unhoused people, farmers, and more. René has lived and worked around the world including in Uganda, Norway, and throughout Southwest Asia. She is the founder and leader of the Boston HUB of the nonprofit Black Girl Environmentalist and on the board of various mission driven organizations including the Boston Public Library Fund's young adult board.
David Queeley (he/him)
Green Jobs Solutions Map Project Manager
With over 26 years working with communities, neighborhoods, funders and with state and federal agencies, David specializes in helping build vision, capacity, and constituencies for equitable and sustainable urban initiatives, helping neighborhoods realize inclusive and equitable outcomes. At TB, he’ll be leading a collaborative project between American Forests, PowerCorpsBOS and TB called Green Jobs Solution Map. Incorporating STEW-MAP tools from the US Forest Service, the project will identify collaborators, diagram solutions to barriers, and identify green career pathways across metro Boston.
Joshua Reed (he/him)
Education Manager
Josh is interested in environmental and economic justice policy issues and passionate about volunteerism. He believes strongly in educating youth on environmental justice and sustainable development. Previous to TB, he served in the Peace Corps in Comoros as a TEFL Volunteer and also joined local Comorians in various sustainable environmental projects. In AmeriCorps he served as a Volunteer Manager for the nonprofit Opportunity Living. He is a lover of nature and explores new hiking and camping spots throughout New England. He recently completed his MA in Applied Politics at Suffolk University.
Marco Lomax (he/him)
Tree Care Coordinator
Marco is a recent Northeastern University graduate, holding Master of Science in Environmental Science and Policy, as well as a Bachelor of Science in Environmental and Sustainability Sciences. A lifelong lover of the outdoors, he has worked as a landscaper and gardener since he was in middle school. He has also worked as a science educator, leading science curriculum development at multiple summer camps. As an undergrad, he worked as a seasonal staff with Boston Parks and Recreation’s Urban Wilds department, planting trees, managing invasive species, and educating volunteers and students about the benefits of native biodiversity. He returned to the Urban Wilds in 2025 to lead the Southwest Boston CDC’s Green Team to restore a large swath of Sherrin Woods in Hyde Park through invasive species management, trail building, and tree care. In his free time, Marco loves riding his bike around Boston, hiking, rock climbing, and cooking.
