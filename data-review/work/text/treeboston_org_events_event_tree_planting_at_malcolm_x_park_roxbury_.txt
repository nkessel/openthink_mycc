Tree Planting At Malcolm X Park - Roxbury - Tree Boston
Come Plant Trees with Tree Boston!
In partnership with the Arbor Day Foundation and the NBA, Tree Boston will be planting 9 brand new trees at Malcolm X Park in Roxbury, Massachusetts.
We are excited to engage with Roxbury residents, organizations, and local leaders in expanding Boston’s urban forest through planting trees.
