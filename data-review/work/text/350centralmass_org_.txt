​350 Central Mass Is an Affiliate of 350 Mass
350 Mass
is a statewide, grassroots, member-led, campaign-focused network committed to a just transition for a climate-safe world. 350 Mass is part of the Better Future Project.
As a statewide organization, 350 Mass works on climate justice with a focus on Massachusetts, and when applicable, coordinates with regional and national efforts. Members are interested in different facets of the climate mission, from electrifying transportation to climate legislation to coalition-building for a powerful movement. The group’s work includes mobilization efforts, educational forums, protests and standouts, lobbying for legislation, a robust media presence and direct-action tactics.

Worcester Telegram
, Dec. 14, 2021
WORCESTER — Climate activists from the group 350 Central Mass protested along Shrewsbury Street Tuesday afternoon in the ongoing fight against Enbridge's Line 3 pipeline carrying crude oil from Canada into the Midwest.
The activists said they are renewing calls to the US Army Corps of Engineers to order a federal Environmental Impact Statement. (EIS).
​
The project, which started a year ago, was completed despite stiff opposition and environmentalists have continued the fight even though the project has been completed. Line 3 starts in Alberta, Canada, and clips a corner of North Dakota before crossing Minnesota en route to Enbridge’s terminal in Superior, Wisconsin.
​
Then, on Dec. 14, we delivered a
letter
to Rep. James McGovern at his Worcester office, demanding an EIS for Line 3.

Fix the Grid
is a grassroots campaign in the six Northeast states that aims to accelerate a just transition to a democratic, transparent, and renewable electric grid. The coalition pushes regional energy regulators, including

, to incentivize clean energy like wind, solar, and storage, instead of keeping us hooked on fossil fuels, which exacerbate the climate emergency and harm our most vulnerable communities. Please lend your support to this crucial campaign! Learn more at
