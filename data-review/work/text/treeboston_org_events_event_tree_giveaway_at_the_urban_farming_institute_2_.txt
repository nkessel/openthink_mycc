Tree Giveaway at The Urban Farming Institute - Tree Boston
Hey Mattapan! Free trees are coming your way!
Tree Boston is teaming up once again with the Urban Farming Institute to give away 25 FREE trees to local residents.
Want one? We strongly recommend pre-registering using the form below. Trees will be available first come, first served starting at 10 AM, with priority given to Mattapan and Boston residents.
We’ll have a beautiful mix of trees available, including but subject to change:
Serviceberry
Magnolia
Red Maple
Not sure which tree is right for your yard? No worries. Our Tree Team will be there to help you learn more about each species and choose the best fit for your space.
Come grab a tree, meet your neighbors, and help us grow a greener Mattapan!
