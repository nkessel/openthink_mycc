Welcome to
Climate Action Now!
Climate Action Now of Western Massachusetts is a grassroots, all-volunteer group. Our purpose is to help build a powerful climate justice movement through organizing, action and public education.
About Us
Taking Action
Join the Global Grassroots Movement.
Climate Action Now was created in the summer of 2012 as a community response to the climate catastrophe. We are dedicated to building a powerful movement for climate justice through organizing, action, and public education.
We invite everyone who wants to help build a vibrant movement for climate justice to join us.
