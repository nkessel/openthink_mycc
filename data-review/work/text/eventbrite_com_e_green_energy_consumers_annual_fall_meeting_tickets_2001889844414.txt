Overview
Join us as we celebrate our progress toward a zero-carbon future and prepare for the challenges that lie ahead.
This year's annual meeting focuses on practical ways we can all be smarter energy consumers and more engaged citizens.
We'll be showcasing emerging clean energy technologies, including window heat pumps, portable batteries, smart heat pump water heaters, and plug-in solar. Everyone loves a good show-and-tell.
You can also get an insider update on the 2026 energy bill, our 2027 legislative agenda, and information about what’s happening in the executive branch.
Tickets are $10, and all are welcome.
And there will be tacos!
