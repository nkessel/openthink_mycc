No Clearcut for Profit

learcutting
In this one project,
E
versource plans to clear-cut 278 acres
to widen their powerline corridor through 11 towns. Instead, they should cut only as much as is necessary to protect the powerlines. They have not used due care to distinguish between trees that pose risks and those that do not.
Project name:
WT-11 Transmission Right-of-Way Reliability Program (TRRP) Project
Towns impacted:
Northfield, Erving, Wendell, Montague, Leverett, Shutesbury, Pelham, Belchertown, Amherst, Granby, and Ludlow
