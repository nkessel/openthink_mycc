We Don't Just Take It, We Take Charge!
Massachusetts Senior Action Council is a statewide, grassroots, senior-run organization that empowers its members to collectively address key public policy and community issues that affect their health and well-being.
120 Mass Senior Action members at older adult lobby day, raising the senior voice at the statehouse. Members educated their legislators about high healthcare costs and advocated for reforms.
The only organization in the history of the MBTA to successfully lower the cost of a fare! In 2024, successful advocated to win the first reduced para transit fare in the Nation!
