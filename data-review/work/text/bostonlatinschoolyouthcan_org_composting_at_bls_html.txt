Composting at BLS!
The BLS Composting Program
BLS is the second Boston Public School to pioneer an
in-school composting program!
Partnering with
Black Earth Compost
, we've provided students with a composting option to keep leftover food from going to waste! Did you know that, i
n the United States,
people waste
80 million tons of food every year
, which equals 149 billion meals?
Composting
allows us to
return those nutrients and carbon to the soil to improve soil quality, support plant growth and build resilience in our local ecosystems and communities, as well
lowering greenhouse gases
by improving carbon sequestration in the soil and by preventing methane emissions through aerobic decomposition!
Help your school community by volunteering to become a
Compost Champion
to keep waste out of our compost bins!
