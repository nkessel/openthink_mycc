Join Green Energy Consumers, New Lynn Coalition, and the City of Lynn at Kings Lynne to learn about energy efficiency and heat pumps.
Whether you’re a tenant, first-time homebuyer, or a landlord, heating and cooling can take up a big portion of your budget, and improving your home's energy efficiency is one way to reduce energy use and save money.
Join us to learn about Mass Save rebates and incentives on energy efficiency upgrades such as insulation, air sealing, and heat pumps. Heat pumps offer an energy-efficient way to heat and cool your home without burning fossil fuels. With the various incentives available, heat pumps can be more affordable than other options, and income-qualified households may even qualify for no-cost upgrades!
If you’re considering replacing an aging oil tank, dealing with frequent boiler or furnace repairs, or paying high energy bills for electric resistance or oil heating, now is a great time to explore your options. Learn how energy efficiency improvements and heat pumps have the potential to save you money, improve home comfort, and reduce your energy use.
The event will cover the following topics:
Energy Efficiency 101: How insulation, air sealing, and weatherization can reduce energy use, lower utility bills, and improve home comfort
Heat Pumps 101: What they are, how they work, their benefits, and upfront and operating costs
Latest information on rebates for energy efficiency upgrades and heat pumps
No-Cost upgrades for income-qualified households and Designated Equity Communities
