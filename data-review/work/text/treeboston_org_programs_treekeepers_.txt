Learn to Care for Trees in Your Community
Our newly redesigned Community TreeKeepers program is here!
Sign up for the second session on March 28, 2026
Local residents who want to care for trees or advocate for tree equity are invited to free community workshops to learn about the preservation, protection, and creation of green spaces in their urban neighborhoods.
We will hold three workshops in winter/spring and three workshops in summer/fall. Workshops will be held at our office in Dorchester, with hands-on training during our public planting events. By the end of this program, you will be confident in your knowledge of trees as public utilities and be able to impact how your friends, family, and neighbors view trees.
Workshops topics will include:
Tree identification
Tree care best practices
Tree Equity
Health assessment of trees
Diseases that affect urban trees
Identification of common invasive species in our region
Over the series of workshops, participants will receive training on tools such as
i-Tree
and
TESA
to show the importance of the urban tree canopy in Boston when developing advocacy and project materials.
