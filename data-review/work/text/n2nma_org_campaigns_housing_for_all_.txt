Housing for All - Neighbor to Neighbor Massachusetts
Share this page:
Statewide
Whether you’re a renter or a homeowner, your home is a place where you should feel safe and secure – it’s the heart of your daily life.
Politicians that cave to corporate interests have paved the way for corporate developers, real estate, and profit-driven landlords to drive up housing costs. The result is a manufactured housing crisis where too many of us struggle to make rent or mortgage payments, while the people who drove prices up blame us for not being able to afford it.
From Springfield to Salisbury, whether you live a triple-decker or a ranch, you should be able to make a comfortable, healthy home in Massachusetts.
We must come together to rewrite the rules, set new terms, and make sure we can stay in our homes and our neighborhoods, no matter our color or how much money we have in our wallets.
