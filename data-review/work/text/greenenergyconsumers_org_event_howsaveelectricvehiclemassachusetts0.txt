How to Save on an Electric Vehicle in Massachusetts
Date
Wednesday, November 18
Time
12:00 pm
Location
Online
RSVP Now
Electric vehicle (EV) drivers can save on lower fuel and maintenance costs, and the state of Massachusetts has a rebate program to bring down the costs of purchasing or leasing a new or used EV. Join this webinar to learn:
How much you can expect to save on fuel and maintenance costs, and how to calculate estimates specific to your situation
How the state MOR-EV program can lower the cost of acquiring a new or used EV, and how to get the rebate at your local dealership or post-purchase
We'll have plenty of time to answer your questions.
