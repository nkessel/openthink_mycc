ICE OUT FOR GOOD
We are opposing ICE’s occupation of our cities because it is self-defense for democracy, and against the same enemies who are driving us headlong into the climate crisis. There is no path to meaningful climate action in a world where a fossil-fuel bought president like Trump has access to his own personal gestapo.

: ICE OUT FOR GOOD
Project Rise Up
Throughout history and around the globe, students and workers have always led the way to topple authoritarianism , using mass non-cooperation to topple dictators. We’re teaming up with workers and unions to organize against the authoritarian overreach of the Trump administration, and training the muscle of mass cooperation towards larger and larger sustained action.
Read more
: Project Rise Up
Electoral Organizing
Sunrise has helped elect hundreds of Green New Deal champions at all levels of government, gotten thousands of politicians to sign the no fossil fuel money pledge, and mobilized our generation to make climate a top issue in our elections.

: Electoral Organizing
Green New Deal for Communities
