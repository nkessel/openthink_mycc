Join us to learn about CRWA's Young Professionals Board, a group of emerging leaders dedicated to supporting a clean, resilient Charles.
Entering its second year, Charles River Watershed Association's Young Professionals Board is a group of emerging leaders dedicated to supporting CRWA’s mission to protect, restore, and enhance the Charles River and its watershed through science, advocacy, and the law.
The purpose of the Young Professionals Board (YPB) is to act as ambassadors for CRWA and support the organization's goals through fundraising, networking opportunities, advocacy, and more. Up to 35 early- and mid-career professionals, ages 25-40, will grow their experience and expertise as leaders in environmental issues in the Greater Boston area. Join us on November 10th to learn more!
