Join CRWA’s Young Professionals Board
Oct 28
Written by
Carly Sherman
CRWA is seeking passionate young professionals (ages 25 - 40) to join our
Young Professionals Board
for its second year. As a YPB member, you’ll help drive CRWA’s fundraising and outreach by running fundraising campaigns, hosting community events, and cultivating the support needed for local climate resilience and watershed restoration work. Along the way, you’ll get hands-on board experience, learn directly from local environmental leaders, and connect with other young professionals across Boston who care about our local environment.
Join us on November 10th to learn more!
Applications open November 9th.
