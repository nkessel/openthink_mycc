Come see us at the City of Medford's annual Harvest Your Energy Festival at Riverbend Park. This event is a perfect opportunity for businesses, residents, and organizations to come together and learn about energy savings, environmentally friendly practices, and innovative climate change solutions!
The festival includes exhibits and activities from a number of organizations in the area, as well as opportunities to buy environmentally themed art from local artists. Festival goers can enjoy food, live music, and tours of the wind turbine.
