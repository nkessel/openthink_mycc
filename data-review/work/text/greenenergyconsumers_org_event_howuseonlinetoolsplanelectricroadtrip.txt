Planning a road trip in an electric vehicle (EV) is a little different than planning a road trip in a gas car, because you need to plan in time to charge. The good news is: many modern EVs have that kind of planning capability built right into their navigation systems, and there are great online tools to help you plan! Join this half-hour webinar to get introduced to two online tools we love, PlugShare and A Better Route Planner. We'll demonstrate how to use them and answer your questions!
