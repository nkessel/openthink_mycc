The Next Generation Caring for Boston’s Urban Forest
Our
Teen Urban Tree Corps (TUTC)
program provides paid work and a chance to explore environmental careers for Boston teens, aged 14-18. Participants gain hands-on experience through tree care, field trips, and talks from professionals in green jobs. There are two paid sessions each year: the
s
ummer program
and the
school-year
program
.
Both programs engage with local youth organizations through
learning exchanges
. These exchanges allow Boston youth to share the work they do and broaden their network within and across their neighborhoods.
Through field trips
to locations such as the Harvard Forest, Arnold Arboretum, and the Boston Nature Center participants learn from professionals working in green spaces across Massachusetts.
Learn more about our Summer Program
The summer program meets for
25 hours/week
and youth will be paid
$15/hour
. The program lasts
seven weeks
from July to August. Participants will self-select into three separate crews: tree care, research, and outreach. The tree care crew is responsible for watering approximately 150 trees each week in the Fields Corner area. The research crew is responsible for conducting air-quality and surface-temperature research at multiple sites in Fields Corner. And the outreach crew learns how to conduct community outreach both in-person and through social media.
Learn more about our School-Year Program
The school-year program meets for approximately
10 hours/week
and youth will be paid
$15/hour
. The program lasts
seven months
from October to May. Participants assist staff at community tree planting events, develop social media posts and in-person workshops, support advocacy efforts and plan our annual Teen Tree Summit.
